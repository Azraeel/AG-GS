## Fixed Canon Spine

- The Avant Great War ran from 17 August 1937 to 3 December 1949.
- The war was fought between the United Alignment of Nations and the Imperial Pact.
- The Vesperan Empire was the central Imperial Pact power and collapsed in 1949.
- The war caused 105-128 million deaths, with civilians making up more than half.
- The Continuation War ran from 3 December 1949 to 6 February 1951, involving rogue Vesperan military elements in places including Volgastan, Khalindar, and Mariposia.
- The Solaran-Khalindarian Conference of 1940 was held in Congrave and secured Khalindar's support against the Vesperan Empire through territorial, economic, infrastructure, and military promises.
- The old source label `Western Alliance` is not current canon. Use `United Alignment of Nations` for the Great War coalition unless the user creates a more specific Solaran-led bloc later.
- After the Avant Great War, Solara did not deliver the full Khalindarian settlement. Old source says disputed promised lands were absorbed into the Buda Republic and Belcanto Nations.
- Khalindar repeatedly demanded reparations and settlement enforcement. Solara dismissed those demands, creating the direct grievance that led to the Solara-Khalindar War.
- Solara was nationally united and extremely confident before the 1990 war; unity and pride fed complacency rather than domestic division.
- Khalindar's home front remained unified during the Solara-Khalindar War despite severe economic strain.
- AGGPI was formed in November 1994 by Solara and the now-defunct Union of Zhensanov as a late-war/immediate settlement deterrent framework for the Solara-Khalindar War. Old references to a 1995 foundation should be treated as charter/operational formalization unless the user says otherwise.

## Canon Conflicts To Review

- `Western Alliance`: Old pages use it for the Solaran side of the Great War. Current canon says it is not a thing. Proposed handling: replace with `United Alignment of Nations` in Great War-era pages.
- `AGGPI 1995 founding`: Old pages say AGGPI was founded in 1995 and list Aurendale, Solara, and Zhensanov as founders. Current canon says November 1994 by Solara and the Union of Zhensanov. Proposed handling: November 1994 founding conference, 1995 charter/operational expansion. Aurendale can be treated as an early member only if the user approves.
- `Imperial Council`: Old Vesperan pages use Imperial Council as a Vesperan advisory/legislative institution. User canon also uses the Imperial Council as Khalindar's ministerial body. Proposed handling: create an `Imperial Council` disambiguating institution page with Vesperan and Khalindarian sections, then later split if needed.
- `Solaran-Khalindarian Confederence`: Old page title is misspelled. Proposed handling: publish the corrected page title `Solaran-Khalindarian Conference of 1940` and create an alias/redirect for the old spelling.
- `Khalindar settlement geography`: Old source names the Buda Republic and Belcanto Nations as recipients of promised lands. The exact red-line geography from the 1940 conference still needs map-level confirmation.
